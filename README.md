# rust_music
Rust_boy music app, specifically lossless quality audio, keeping your music alive in the wasteland!
.flac

Music, Maps, Messaging all in one platform, without the bloatware. 
This is your music app, but in rust.
It is designed to be super lightweight, allowing you to carry only the music you want, when you want it. 

Multiple means of deployment, localized storage or disjointed.
